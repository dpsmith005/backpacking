Liquid fertilizer
1 gallon rain water
1 T wood ash
1 t Epsom salt
1 T molasses (Black strap)
2-3 cups Black Cow compost (in mesh bag)
1 t yeast
leaf mold (from base of big old tree in the woods)
Ferment 3 days using fish aeration pump.
Add 1 part to 5 parts water ( 3 cups to 1 gallon)

JADAM Liquid fertilizer

Mid season plant boost
use organic bananas.  Place in blender with water.
Pour in 5 gallon bucket
1 cup Blackstrap molasses (unsulfured)
3 cups lactic acid serum
Let ferment 2 weeks or until every thing is dissolved, then use.

Lactic Acid Bacteria (LAB) serum - fermented rice water and milk
cover rice with water and let sit.  drain rice water.  Make 1 quart.
Ferment the rice water 3-5 days.  Will have bubbles and a film on top with a smell of yeast.
Add to 1 gallon of whole milk and let sit 3-5 days.
strain off the liquid
eat the solids (cheese)
Dilute 1 oz to 1 gallon water

Liquid Nitrogen Fertilizer (3-6 months fermentation)
5 gallon bucket with lid
leaf mold
urine
The longer the fermentation the better the fertilizer
Dilute 1 oz to 1 gallon of rain water before adding to garden
This becomes aged urea

Fish Fertilizer

fish

Dilute 1 oz to 1 gallon of rain water before adding to garden

Fall Soil boost
1 gallon rain water
10 T hydrogen Peroxide
Dig trench and bury dead plants.  Add some compost. Add some of the peroxide mixture.  This boosts oxygen and aids in decomposition
